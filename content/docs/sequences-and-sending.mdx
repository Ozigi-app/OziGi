A sequence is the series of touches a lead receives. Email is the channel with steps in it; LinkedIn is a single action, and the two are set up separately.

## How the two channels run

Email and LinkedIn are configured as **separate campaigns**. A new outreach campaign carries email steps only; LinkedIn gets its own campaign from the LinkedIn tab, sourcing its own leads. They do not share a lead list, and nothing interleaves into a single queue.

```diagram
 day 0        day 3          day 7
   │            │              │
EMAIL CAMPAIGN ────────────────────────────▶
   ├─ Cold intro
   │            ├─ Follow-up
   │            │              ├─ Breakup

LINKEDIN CAMPAIGN ─────────────────────────▶
   ├─ Connection request
        (one action — nothing follows it)
```

Older campaigns created before the split may still hold both email and LinkedIn steps on one lead list. Those keep running as configured; you just cannot build that shape any more.

## Email steps

A typical three-step sequence:

| Step | Delay | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Cold intro | day 0 | One specific observation about them, one sentence on why you are writing, one small ask |
| Follow-up | +3 days | New angle, not a reminder. "Just bumping this" is worse than silence |
| Breakup | +4 days | Short, no guilt, easy to reply to |

Each email is generated per lead using your persona and whatever Ozigi knows about that person. It is personalisation from real signal — their repositories, their writing, their stack — rather than mail-merged first names.

Use **Preview Emails** on the campaign page before every send after changing anything. It generates three samples against real leads.

## The LinkedIn action

LinkedIn actions run through the [browser extension](/docs/linkedin-extension), from your own logged-in tab.

**Connection request with a personalised note.** This is the entire LinkedIn channel — one action, written per lead.

Ozigi does not send LinkedIn DMs of any kind: no follow-up after a connection is accepted, no Open Profile messages, no cold DMs. Sequencing on LinkedIn is what gets accounts restricted, so the channel does one thing and stops. Multi-step follow-up belongs on email, where you control the delays and the domain.

## Daily limits

Set a per-day cap per campaign — 40 emails and 20 LinkedIn connection requests by default. Sends stop when the cap is reached and resume the next day, picking up where they left off.

LinkedIn has a second cap on top of that, set in the [extension](/docs/linkedin-extension) itself, which applies across every campaign at once. Whichever is lower is what you actually get.

The caps protect two different things. On email it is your domain reputation — a new domain sending two hundred cold emails on day one gets classified as spam and stays that way. On LinkedIn it is your account standing, and LinkedIn's tolerance is considerably lower than most people assume.

> **Start lower than feels productive.** Twenty to thirty emails a day from a warmed domain is a reasonable opening position. LinkedIn connection requests should be lower still. Ramping slowly costs a few weeks; getting your domain filtered costs months.

If you need more volume than one inbox supports, Pro allows connecting additional inboxes to spread the load.

## Reply detection

When a lead replies, their sequence pauses automatically and their status moves to `replied`. Only that lead stops — the campaign continues for everyone else.

This matters more than it sounds. The fastest way to lose a warm reply is to send them your scheduled follow-up the next morning as though they had said nothing.

Replies on either channel pause the lead across both channels.

## Sending infrastructure

Outbound email sends from **your** connected inbox, not from Ozigi's servers. Consequences worth knowing:

- Replies arrive in your normal mailbox, and the thread looks like any other conversation.
- Deliverability is tied to your domain and your sending history.
- Your provider's own daily limits still apply on top of Ozigi's cap. Gmail in particular enforces its own ceiling.
- Sent mail appears in your Sent folder.

Newsletters are a separate system that sends from Ozigi's infrastructure to an opted-in list. Do not route cold outreach through it — see [Newsletter](/docs/newsletter).

## CRM sync

When a lead is first contacted on either channel, it is pushed to your connected CRM as a new contact. Sync happens on first contact, not at sourcing, so your CRM does not fill up with people you never actually approached.

Setup: [Integrations](/docs/integrations).

## When sends do not go out

Check in this order:

1. **Is an inbox connected and active?** [Email Setup](/docs/email-setup).
2. **Is the daily cap already reached?** Sends resume the following day.
3. **Are any leads actually due?** A step with a three-day delay has nobody due until day three.
4. **Is the campaign paused?**
5. **Did leads clear the 0.3 score cut-off?** Anything scoring under it is discarded during sourcing and never reaches the campaign. See [Campaigns](/docs/campaigns).

For LinkedIn specifically, sends require the extension installed, enabled, and a LinkedIn tab open. See [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting).
